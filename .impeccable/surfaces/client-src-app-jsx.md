# Surface brief: CV Tailor app (all routes)

Scope: whole client app. Visitor mode: Operate (task: paste CV and posting, run, copy). Routes: Tailor, History, Templates, Analytics, Admin. Replacement visual world; old glass/blob look is the anti-reference. Floating Tweaks panel retired; light/dark and EN/ES stay.

Audience and job: job applicants applying to one posting, bilingual. Action: Tailor, then copy bullets/letter. Constraints: honest fit score (keyword coverage), no invented proof, both languages, sandboxed styled-CV iframe untouched.

Memorable moment: the result prints as colour copies of one form. Bullets on white, cover letter on canary, raw text on rose, design on sky, each tab a sheet edge. Fit lands as a rubber stamp.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One application, written once, struck through to every copy. Refuses the glass-blob AI dashboard and the neutral SaaS card grid; every surface is a ruled form or a coloured copy sheet.

OWN-WORLD: Cool white stock, canary, rose and sky copy sheets as flat fields, on a quiet cool-grey desk. Printed black ink for labels, carbon-blue for typed content and the primary action, serial red only for serial numbers and stamps. 2-3px radii, 1px ink rules, perforation dashes, ruled typing lines. Public Sans for printed labels and text, Courier Prime for typed fields. Dark theme is carbon: charcoal sheets, dimmed stock tints, chalk-blue ink.

STORY: The applicant understands in one glance where the CV goes, where the posting goes and what Tailor does; they believe the output because the rewrite is shown next to the original at one scale and the fit is labelled as keyword coverage; they copy from the right copy sheet and leave.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim top bar with the new sheet-stack mark, wordmark, ruled nav tabs, EN/ES and theme boxes. Below, a pad header (title, tagline, three-box progress strip, fit stamp when present), then two equal ruled forms side by side (CV left, posting right, 1fr/1fr, typing lines, perforated count stub), the order block under them with style and tone check boxes and the carbon-blue TAILOR button at the right foot, and the history stubs beneath.

FORM: Triplicate order pad, assigned index 5 of the grounded list (proofreader's galley was index 1, the pick), seed key 8b4f3aed. Raises: serial numbers on every history artifact; original and rewrite at one scale on a shared baseline; flat unmodulated stock fields owning whole regions.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
